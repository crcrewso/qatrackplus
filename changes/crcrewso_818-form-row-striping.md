* **Appearance**: Long forms, and the QC tables on the perform and review pages, are easier to read a single row across: their rows now alternate between the page background and a faint grey. Two things move. Form rows gain a few pixels of padding, so their text sits inside the tint rather than on its edge; and on a form that is not laid out in Bootstrap's horizontal style - the backup-site form is the one in QATrack+ - the row now encloses its columns, where before the tint sat above the field instead of behind it. **The highlight on the row you are entering has been darkened slightly**, from `#eeeeee` to `#e8e8e8`, so that it stays clearly ahead of the shading without closing on the grey box that marks a calculated value. Nothing else about colour changes: the pass, tolerance and action backgrounds are untouched, a calculated or skipped value keeps its own box, and a disabled field on a shaded row keeps looking disabled. The sign-in page is not shaded, having nothing to group ([#818](https://github.com/qatrackplus/qatrackplus/issues/818)).
